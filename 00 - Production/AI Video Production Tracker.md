> [!important] Calibration left Scene 12 entirely, and it left with no voices
> [[12 - Below Expectations]] was rewritten: its second movement is now a **phone call between the two live actors**, and calibration moved to a wordless screen prologue at the top of [[13 - The Performance Review]]. Three assets go with it. `AIV-065` (People Partner) stays **retired**; `AIV-063` (Kristina, calibration) and `AIV-064` (Kara, calibration) are now **retired too** — the prologue has no voice-over at all, so their two passes become on-screen comment text inside `AIV-066`, which itself moves to Scene 13 and loses the calendar invite. Net effect on the shoot: **the whole calibration sequence went from three talking heads to one screen graphic, and Scene 12's back half now needs no asset whatsoever.** The reasoning is in the two scenes' notes — putting the decision in a room invites the audience to audit the room, and a document filling in for three minutes is a rehearsal risk that a one-minute cold open isn't.
