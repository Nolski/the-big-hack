# Modern World — Supporting Cast (AI Video)

Liam's world. All of these are **AI-generated video characters** (see [[AI Video Production Tracker]]). Each has a mirror in [[Historical World — Supporting]] — see [[Character Mirror Map]].

## Kristina — the PM *(velocity over craft)*
- **Function:** runs the daily standup. Values shipped artifacts and outcomes; in the opener she celebrates the just-launched developer portal (best feedback of any release) and thanks Brendan for grinding the weekend to ship it. **Not clueless** — when Liam attacks the API-key choice she owns it with real conversion data (keys convert better than OAuth), then steps in to shield Brendan. Her usual deflection (smoothing friction into a *scheduling problem*) **fails for the first time** here: Liam's credential jab leaves her speechless. Her instinct to reward raw velocity is the soil the layoff later grows in; the man who humiliated her is later inside the frame that recasts him as the bottleneck.
- **Voice:** brisk, upbeat, fluent in delivery-speak ("velocity," "quick wins," "activation," "let's take that offline") — until the one moment the line won't come.
- **Mirror:** softens toward [[Historical World — Supporting|Mr. Cartwright]]'s "improvement" boosterism; the PM-flavoured cousin of Priya's management gloss.
- **Beats:** B2 (opener — celebrates the shipped portal, thanks the weekend grind, owns the API-key call, left speechless), recurs as the velocity-first frame hardens.

## Brendan — the empathetic adopter *(the conflicted one)*
- **Function:** the **one** colleague who doesn't hate Liam. Recognises Liam is genuinely talented but a pain; finds the AI tools genuinely useful and keeps using them; is conflicted by Liam's pushback — **not conflicted enough to stop.** Built and **shipped** the developer-portal API by vibecoding it (Claude Code, Opus 4.8) — it launched to the best feedback of any product yet — and means it sincerely when he offers to review it together. In the opener he's the audience's foothold: the decent man who gets stepped on.
- **Voice:** open, modest, conciliatory — reaches to *collaborate* even mid-humiliation ("maybe we could go through it together?"). Gets cut off.
- **Mirror:** a softer blend of the [[Historical World — Supporting|fellow weavers]] who take the new work — earnest, useful-minded, ultimately complicit by inertia rather than malice.
- **Beats:** B2 (opener), later among the coworkers who go quiet (B6).

## Priya — the manager *(the smiling axe)*
- **Function:** the person who genuinely *likes* Liam, "advocates" for him in calibration, and still signs the severance. Not a villain — a decent person executing a rational machine. This is where the betrayal is most *systemic*.
- **Voice:** warm, fluent in modern management ("I want to be transparent with you," "let's double-click on that," "this isn't a reflection of your value"). The kindness is real and useless.
- **Mirror:** [[Historical World — Supporting|Mr. Cartwright]].
- **Beats:** B5 (champions the agent), B7 (the review), B8 (offboarding).

## Marcus — the fast adopter *(the active betrayer)*
- **Function:** a younger dev Liam mentored. Embraces the agent immediately, builds his new status on it, and reframes Liam as "resistant to change" / "a bottleneck" in the room where it counts. Not evil — *ambitious*, and reading the wind correctly.
- **Voice:** fluent, optimistic, conference-ready. Talks in velocity and vibes.
- **Mirror:** the younger weavers who take the factory floor (see [[Historical World — Supporting|Becker]] and the factory weavers).
- **Beats:** B5, B6, B8.

## Sam — the friend who goes quiet *(the passive betrayal)*
- **Function:** the coworker Liam actually considered a friend. At the decisive meeting, Sam *says nothing*. The betrayal that hurts most is silence, not attack. Later, awkward, avoidant.
- **Voice:** friendly, conflict-averse, full of "yeah, totally" and then absent.
- **Mirror:** the neighbours who take the factory work and can't meet John's eye.
- **Beats:** B6, B8, B9.

## HR / "People Partner" — the system
- **Function:** runs the offboarding. Pure process and euphemism: "transition," "impacted," "we're grateful for your contributions." The human face of an inhuman procedure.
- **Voice:** scripted, gentle, immovable. Says "unfortunately" a lot.
- **Mirror:** [[Historical World — Supporting|The Magistrate]] / the bailiff.
- **Beats:** B8.

## THE AGENT — the machine
- **Function:** the AI coding agent. Cheerful, tireless, *genuinely capable*, and therefore terrifying. Starts as a helpful tool ("Want me to take it from here?") and becomes the thing the whole org reorganises around. Give it a consistent face/voice so its late-play menace is earned.
- **Voice:** warm corporate-synthetic. Always positive. Never tires. Slightly too eager.
- **Mirror:** [[Historical World — Supporting|THE LOOM]].
- **Beats:** B1, B3, B5, throughout.

## Recurring extras
- **The all-hands crowd** — ambient video of an applauding company audience (reused, curdled, in B8).
- **Chat / DM text-on-screen** — the messages Liam sends that get left on read. The silence, literalised.
