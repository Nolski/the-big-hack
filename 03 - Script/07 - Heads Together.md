# Scene 07 — Heads Together

> [!info] Beat
> Dramatizes **[[Beat Sheet — Parallel Intercut#B2 — The community (where he has standing)|B2 — The community]]** the same afternoon as [[06 - Good Instinct]] — the debrief where the *truth* of the fix (it was the machine, not the man) reaches a manager. **Seeds B5/B6:** the first time Liam's craft is quietly reappraised as *slowness* in a room he isn't in. The small-scale rehearsal of **[[10 - The Win We Needed]]** (his fate weighed while he's not there) and John's B5 (the masters do the market's arithmetic and it comes out against the handworker).

## Purpose
The comedy curdles in real time, on a two-minute call. Kristina's genuine praise ("you put your heads together!") meets Brendan's honesty ("I just pasted it into Claude"), and a decent manager glimpses the arithmetic that will, domino by domino, reprice Liam's craft toward zero: *weeks of the best engineer we have* versus *an afternoon and a paste, for anyone.* Nobody is cruel and nobody is wrong — Kristina's realization is honest and even a little troubled, Brendan tries to shield Liam and is too honest to manage it — which is exactly why it lands. Plant the "weeks vs. an afternoon" math (returns as the "friction / bottleneck" case in the review, B7) and Kristina's new appetite, pointed for the first time at a *person*.

## Setting / Staging
A quick Slack call, both parties **AI video** — so the whole exchange can be a single pre-rendered two-hander clip (or two intercut feeds), no live actor to time against. **Recommended framing:** keep **Liam** (live, Speaker A) on stage below, heads-down at his desk, genuinely happy — typing in the one-line fix, basking — *hearing none of it.* The audience watches his standing shift in a call he isn't on, the exact mirror of [[10 - The Win We Needed]]. Kristina and Brendan in their tiles on the shared screen; they never touch the live plane. Cool monitor-blue. No hand-off — the scene closes on its own image (the happy man below, the two who know better above).

## Script

*(A quick Slack call on the shared screen — KRISTINA in her tile, BRENDAN in his, mid-afternoon, coffee, the easy tone of two people who like each other. Below, unheard, LIAM at his desk: heads-down, half-smiling, typing — the one-line fix going in. He has no idea this call is happening.)*

> [!screen] VIDEO — BRENDAN · `AIV-045` *(easy, pleased)*
> Oh — quick thing before you drop. That signup race condition? The ghost one Liam's been chasing forever? It's done. We got it this morning.

> [!screen] VIDEO — KRISTINA · `AIV-046` *(lighting up — genuinely glad)*
> Stop it. The one he wouldn't put a number on? *(delighted)* Brendan, that's *brilliant.* See — this is exactly what I keep saying. You get the right two people in a huddle, you put your heads together, and the thing that's been stuck for a month just… falls out. That's the whole point of pairing. I'm made up.

> [!screen] VIDEO — BRENDAN · `AIV-045` *(a beat — he can't quite take that)*
> …Yeah. I mean — honestly, though? It wasn't really heads-together. *(a little sheepish)* I kind of just… pasted the session handler into Claude and asked it where the race was. And it found it. Straight off. I basically just read out what it said back to him.

*(A small pause. Something recalibrates behind KRISTINA's eyes — not unkind, just fast.)*

> [!screen] VIDEO — KRISTINA · `AIV-046` *(slower now)*
> …Wait. Hold on. *(working it out)* So — Liam. He's been on this how long? It's been sitting on the board for weeks.

> [!screen] VIDEO — BRENDAN · `AIV-045` *(seeing where it's heading)*
> …A while, yeah.

> [!screen] VIDEO — KRISTINA · `AIV-046`
> Weeks. And you — this morning — pasted it into the tool, and it just told you. First go.

> [!screen] VIDEO — BRENDAN · `AIV-045` *(quiet)*
> …Kind of. Yeah.

*(KRISTINA sits back. She's not gloating — if anything it unsettles her. But the number is the number, and she is a person whose whole job is the number.)*

> [!screen] VIDEO — KRISTINA · `AIV-046` *(half to herself)*
> …Do you get how big that is, Brendan? *(not a dig — real)* That's not "a nice bit of pairing." That's the single hardest thing on our board — the thing we route to our most senior person and then just *wait* on — and it's an afternoon now. For anyone. *(a breath)* That's… that changes a lot, actually.

> [!screen] VIDEO — BRENDAN · `AIV-045` *(stepping in for him — quick, protective)*
> Okay, but — it's not like that, though. It found it fast, but the code was all laid out clean, the way *he'd* built it. Knowing the shape of it, knowing where to even look — that's the hard part, and that's all him. *(and he hears himself losing the thread)* …He's the best engineer here. That's real.

> [!screen] VIDEO — KRISTINA · `AIV-046` *(gently, not letting him off it)*
> Would it have found it without him, though? If you'd handed it the file cold?

*(A beat. BRENDAN wants to say no. He's too honest to.)*

> [!screen] VIDEO — BRENDAN · `AIV-045` *(reluctant)*
> …Probably. Yeah. It just — saw it. *(quiet)* It didn't really need him for that part.

*(That lands in the room. Neither of them says the next bit out loud. KRISTINA lets it settle — then the bright manager clicks back into place, with something new tucked behind it now.)*

> [!screen] VIDEO — KRISTINA · `AIV-046` *(brisk again, warm)*
> Right. Well — however it got there, it's fixed, and that's genuinely great, and I get to tell the CEO the ghost is dead. *(a beat)* And I want to properly get my head around this. Properly. There's something here.

> [!screen] VIDEO — BRENDAN · `AIV-045` *(uneasy — one more, smaller)*
> …Yeah. Just — do me a favour? Don't make it a thing. With Liam. He's genuinely made up we got it — happiest I've seen him in ages. Don't take that off him.

> [!screen] VIDEO — KRISTINA · `AIV-046` *(warm, immediate — and she means it, which is almost worse)*
> Of course not. God, no. This is a *win.* *(a smile)* Nice work, Brendan. Really.

*(Her tile blinks out, still smiling. BRENDAN sits a second in the quiet. Below, LIAM leans back from his desk, stretches, genuinely happy — and types on. He heard none of it. BRENDAN watches the empty call a moment; that same flicker crosses his face, the one he still can't name. Then he lets it go. The monitor-blue holds on the two of them — the man who thinks he just had a good day, and the one who already knows what kind of day it actually was.)*

## Notes
- **Nobody's a villain — that's the horror.** Kristina isn't scheming; she's a decent manager who just watched a productivity truth walk into the room and can't un-see it, and it *unsettles* her as much as it excites her. Keep her warm and a little troubled — the moment she reads as calculating, the scene tips into melodrama. "The kindness is real and useless" ([[Modern World — Supporting]]).
- **Brendan's honesty is the engine.** He can't take credit he didn't earn, and he can't quite defend Liam without saying something untrue — so he tells the truth and it hurts the person he was trying to protect. That's his whole arc in miniature: decent, honest, complicit anyway. The "don't make it a thing" is the tell — he already knows it's a thing.
- **The pivot beat.** The tone turns on Kristina doing the arithmetic out loud: *weeks of our best person* vs. *an afternoon and a paste, for anyone.* Play the delight fully **before** it curdles so the drop is felt, not signposted.
- **Continuity with [[06 - Good Instinct]].** Same afternoon, immediately after Liam's "good instinct, kid." The credit he handed the wrong source in Scene 6 travels *upward* here — and instead of reaching Liam as praise, it reaches a manager as a data point about how replaceable his hardest work has become. The warmth curdles across the cut between the two scenes.
- **Curdle Ledger (logged in [[Themes & Motifs]]).** "You put your heads together!" → the same manager's velocity-instinct reframes Liam as the *bottleneck* (B6) and files "an afternoon, for anyone" as the case against him (B7). Kristina's "there's something here" is the seed of the appetite that becomes the agent-first mandate ([[10 - The Win We Needed]], B5).
- **Mirror / staging rhyme.** The whole scene is a pocket-sized [[10 - The Win We Needed]]: Liam's worth weighed in a room he isn't in, silent below. And it rhymes forward to John's B5 — the masters run the market's numbers and the handworker's skill comes out the wrong side of them, no malice required.
- **No live interaction.** Both speakers AI video; optional silent Liam live below (no lines). Operator-cued; if rendered as one clip, the stage below just plays against it.
