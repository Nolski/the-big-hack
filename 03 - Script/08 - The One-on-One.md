# Scene 08 — The One-on-One

> [!info] Beat
> Kristina's regular 1:1 with **Kara**, Head of Product — the first time the new velocity is visible from one level up, and the first time the audience meets **the CEO's company-wide memo on adopting AI**. Sits between [[07 - Heads Together]] (Kristina: "I get to tell my boss we have an extra engineer") and [[10 - The Win We Needed]] (the story travels upstairs anyway). **Seeds B6/B7:** Kara's warning — "one engineer has a good weekend and by Friday it's an operating model" — is exactly what happens.

## Purpose
The middle layer of the company meets the momentum. Kara is the last skeptic with power: she likes the CEO, distrusts his enthusiasms, and finds the AI-generated PRDs flooding her inbox both funny and useless. Kristina, one scene after learning the truth about the signup fix, chooses the safest version of the story — "the team adopted some new tooling" — and watches her own month-old jokes come out of her boss's mouth. The scene ends with Kristina rereading the memo she once screenshotted to mock, and taking it seriously. Nobody is wrong here either: Kara's caution is sane, and it will not matter.

## Setting / Staging
A remote one-on-one, late afternoon: two tiles on the shared screen, Kristina in one, and in the other her boss Kara, the Head of Product. Coffee, calendars, the easy shorthand of a standing meeting. Both parties **AI video**, the same monitor-blue register as [[07 - Heads Together]]; Kara screen-shares the roadmap mid-scene. **Recommended framing:** the stage below stays empty or holds Liam heads-down at his desk, unaware, per the running mirror. After the call ends, the closing beat is Kristina alone: her inbox, the memo, and the CEO's voice reading his own words over it.

## AI Video Cues
- **[[AI Video Production Tracker|AIV-047]] — Kara (the 1:1):** Head of Product, Kristina's boss, reports to the CEO. Dry, seasoned, unhurried; deadpan about the CEO's weekend projects, dead serious about what gets promised upstairs. Warmth under the dryness — she likes these people.
- **[[AI Video Production Tracker|AIV-048]] — Kristina (the 1:1):** same locked look/voice as `AIV-010`. Brings good news and hedges the explanation; recognizes her own old jokes arriving from the other side of the table and doesn't join in.
- **[[AI Video Production Tracker|AIV-049]] — The CEO (memo voice-over):** voice only, measured, reading his own memo. No new footage needed — text on Kristina's screen carries the visual.

*(She reads the memo all the way through this time.)*

## Notes
- **Kara is sane, and it won't matter.** Her caution — understand it before it becomes policy — is the correct read, delivered too late to the wrong layer. By [[10 - The Win We Needed]] the story has already traveled upstairs as a number, exactly the way she feared.
- **Kristina's hedge is the tell.** One scene after [[07 - Heads Together]] she knows precisely how big this is; "the team adopted some new tooling" is the version that keeps the room calm. Her silence at Kara's CEO joke is the character note: a month ago she made that joke. Don't play it as guilt — play it as recalibration.
- **The memo is introduced here.** First appearance in the show; it must be named in full — the CEO's company-wide memo on adopting AI — before it is ever shorthanded as "the memo." The CEO reads his own words in voice-over; no new footage, just text on her screen.
- **Continuity.** The API service is the developer-portal work from [[01 - Cold Open — The Standup]] (shipped Thursday, partners integrating over the weekend, support quiet). Liam's availability comes from the signup fix in [[06 - Good Instinct]] / [[07 - Heads Together]]. Brendan's method — plan first, read the dangerous parts, own integration tests — is what Kristina watched in [[01b - The Cursor Demo]].
- **No QA beat, deliberately.** Nobody signs anything off; "support has been quiet since launch" is the only verification the company has. That is the joke and the point.
