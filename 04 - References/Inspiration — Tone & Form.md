# Inspiration — Tone & Form

> [!quote] Why it matters for *The Big Hack*
> The reference shelf. Each entry notes *what it solves for this play*. Deep-dive notes exist for the two we mine directly: [[The Weavers — Pulled Passages]], [[Office Space — Pulled Lines]].

## Structure — the escalation engine
- **Sorry to Bother You — Boots Riley (2018) — the ladder to study before writing any new scene.** Reality bends further every act while the protagonist stays recognisable; the absurdity is in the institutions, never in the man. This is the exact contract our second half runs on. See [[Structure & Scene Map]] and [[Rework — The Escalation Pass]] §G.
- **The Lehman Trilogy — Stefano Massini / Ben Power.** Direct-address third person, few performers carrying decades. The mode [[Brendan]] narrates in, and the reason the CFAA, the press and the statutes can be handled in twenty seconds of owned artifice instead of being forced into someone's mouth.
- **The Chicago Seven trial.** Absurdism built the right way: the defence escalates and **the institution keeps responding earnestly**. Escalation with a straight man is the whole technique — and in our version Marcus escalates while Liam is mortified.
- ~~**Braided structure.**~~ Retired with John — kept for the record in [[Braided Structure — Reference]].

## The Luddite material — now Marcus's argument bank (public domain)
- **The Weavers (Die Weber) — Gerhart Hauptmann (1892).** Period voice, hunger, machine-breaking. No longer dramatised — it is the shelf Marcus argues from, and the source of verbatim inserts. → [[The Weavers — Pulled Passages]]. [Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/9971) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Weavers_(play)).
- **"Breaking the Frame: An Interactive Luddite Drama."** Puts the audience in the framework-knitters' dilemma — direct subject match. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5JAWWhPzCsQ).

## The arc — layoffs, betrayal, radicalisation
- **Sweat — Lynn Nottage (2015, Pulitzer).** Deindustrialisation; friends-turned-rivals; rage out of precarity. The emotional/political template for both men's fall in a contemporary register. [TheaterMania](https://www.theatermania.com/news/5-plays-that-inspire-social-change_1750549/).

## Tone — satire that curdles
- **Sorry to Bother You — Boots Riley (2018, film).** Tech/labor satire escalating from funny to surreal-horrifying — the model for "laughs that curdle." [Notes From Below](https://notesfrombelow.org/article/sorry-to-bother-you-tech-worker-organising).
- **Office Space — Mike Judge (1999, film).** Mundane corporate-absurd comedy for Liam's early scenes. → [[Office Space — Pulled Lines]]. [IMSDb](https://imsdb.com/scripts/Office-Space.html).
- **Serious Money — Caryl Churchill (1987).** Savage verse satire of finance — jargon *as* the comedy and the indictment. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serious_Money).

## Staging — live actors + pervasive video (our exact problem)
- **Network — Chayefsky (film) / Lee Hall & Ivo van Hove (stage, 2017).** THE precedent for live performers among omnipresent live video feeds and screens — directly our two-speakers-among-AI-videos challenge. Also media-satire-into-rage. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_(play)) · [Playbill: stage+camera choreography](https://playbill.com/article/watch-the-intricate-stage-and-camera-choreography-that-goes-down-seconds-before-each-performance-of-network).
- **The Lehman Trilogy — Massini / Ben Power (2018).** Few performers fluidly switching narrator↔ character across an epic capitalist sweep — model for minimal live cast carrying scale. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lehman_Trilogy).

## Form — direct address, making the audience think
- **Brecht / Epic Theatre — the Verfremdungseffekt.** Visible narrator, direct address, songs/ placards, breaking immersion so the crowd reasons. Tailor-made for a Kiwicon-stage-as-theatre hybrid. → drives the `*(to audience)*` mode in [[00 - Script Format Guide]]. [Epic theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_theatre).
