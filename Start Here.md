# The Big Hack — Vault Home

> *A Kiwicon talk staged as a play. Two live speakers. A cast of AI-generated video characters. Two men, two centuries, one machine.*

**Logline.** As AI coding agents eat the craft he lives for, a brilliant, prickly software developer named **Liam** is managed out of his job and betrayed by the coworkers he trusted — and radicalises. Two hundred years earlier, **John**, a Yorkshire weaver, watches the power loom do the same to his trade, his town, and his family. Their stories run side by side, beat for beat, until the laughs curdle.

**Form.** ~60 minutes. Darkly comic / satirical. **Braided structure** — stay in one story for a whole movement, build to a near-climax, cut away at the peak, build the other, converge at the end (see [[Braided Structure — Reference]]). Every Liam beat still has a John twin. Two live speakers embody Liam and John; everyone else is an AI-generated video character.
