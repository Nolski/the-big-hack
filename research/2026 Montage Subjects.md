# 2026 montage: subjects

Working list for a silent montage of disasters and bad human experiences that add up to 2026. Compiled 2026-10-23. Not part of the script.

Focus: life at home rather than geopolitics. Who has money and who breathes the fumes, what people lose, what they take to the streets. Aotearoa New Zealand and Australia come first: austerity, racism, AI. Wars and disasters abroad are parked at the bottom.

**Check column:** **V** = checked against news reports when this list was made. **W** = from Wikipedia's 2026 timeline only; confirm the date and numbers before cutting. Numbers are snapshots.

## Making it read without sound

- The chyron and on-screen text do the talking. Prefer clips where the lower third states the event or the number.
- Pick shots where the scale shows: crowds, queues, price signs.
- CCTV, dashcam, and phone footage is silent by nature and needs no apology.
- Date cards work here the same way as the time jumps (3.5 s), if the order matters.
- Juxtaposition is free without sound. A red carpet followed by a food bank line needs no narration.
- Aftermath carries more than the moment of death. Avoid identifiable dead or dying people and any footage from inside a shooting.
