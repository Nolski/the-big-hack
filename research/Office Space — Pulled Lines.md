# Office Space — Pulled Lines

> [!quote] Why it matters for *The Big Hack*
> Tone-calibration for [[Liam]]'s early **corporate-absurd comedy** — the mundane, soul-killing process satire that has to be *funny first* so the [[Themes & Motifs|curdle]] lands later.

**Source:** [IMSDb full script](https://imsdb.com/scripts/Office-Space.html) — © 1999 Mike Judge.
**Brief illustrative quotes only (fair use); do not reproduce at length.**

## Lines (brief, for calibration — don't lift verbatim into the show)
- The **TPS reports / coversheet memo** running gag — the perfect "process for its own sake" device.
- **PETER:** "I have eight different bosses right now!" — the diffusion of accountability.
- The mission-statement banner: **"With every decision you make, ask yourself: Is this good for the company?"** — the cult-of-the-firm line to mirror against "we're a family here."
- **PETER:** "I'd probably say, in a given week, I probably do about fifteen minutes of real, actual work."
- Passive-aggressive management: *"If you could just go ahead and… that'd be great. Mmmm, ok?"*
- Efficiency-consultant deflection: *"We always like to avoid confrontation whenever possible."* — the HR/Bob-Porter energy for the HR Offboarding scene.

## How to use it
- **Study the rhythm, write our own lines.** Liam's world needs *2026* specifics (agents, velocity, calibration), not 1999 ones — see [[AI Coding & Labor]].
- The "is this good for the company?" / banner energy is a strong curdle candidate: funny in Act I, chilling over the layoff in the HR Offboarding scene.
